About this investigation

The case view covers seven Taiwan manufacturers and report-described migrant-worker recruitment fees, debt, and related allegations. It includes worker-origin context and report-named company connections.

The GitHub Pages site is a static launch page. The interactive Python application runs separately on Streamlit Community Cloud; use the Streamlit link above to deploy or open that app.

Evidence limitations

Case details are based on user-supplied material attributed to Transparentem and have not been independently verified by SourceSight. Report-named buyers and affiliations are leads, not confirmed supply-chain edges. Scores are screening aids, not findings of legal liability or forced labor.
